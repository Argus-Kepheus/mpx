Long-term vision

A reusable FOSS process-simulation platform

Multiphysics explores how an integrated process-simulation environment can be developed with a FOSS architecture, open scientific access and explicit separation between physical models, numerical methods and software implementations. The software is the long-term engineering artifact; modelling, numerical, experimental and validation problems encountered along the way are expected to generate independent scientific contributions and publications.

Terminology note

FOSS
Free and Open Source Software: software distributed under licences that grant freedoms to use, study, modify and redistribute the software. Here, “free” refers primarily to those freedoms, not necessarily to zero price.
Simulation
A computational representation of selected physical behaviour under explicit models, assumptions and numerical approximations; it is not a claim of exact reproduction of the real process.
Open access vs. free use
Open access means that scientific material can be publicly read or consulted. It does not, by itself, grant unrestricted reuse; reuse of software, data, text or figures depends on the applicable licence and rights.
Platform
A modular research and software environment in which models, numerical methods, calculation engines, data and workflows can be combined. It does not mean a commercial marketplace or necessarily a hosted online service.

This website presents the publishable scientific state of the Multiphysics project. It does not expose private correspondence, internal researcher profiles or unpublished collaborative material.
